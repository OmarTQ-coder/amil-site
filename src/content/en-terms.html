Welcome to Amil. Please read these terms and conditions carefully before registering or requesting any service through the platform. Creating an account, using the platform, submitting a service request or accepting an offer means you agree to these terms and conditions.

1. Definitions

For these terms, the following words have the meanings given next to them:

  • The platform, or Amil: the app or website that provides a technical service connecting customers with independent service providers.
  • Customer: a person who uses the platform to request a task or service.
  • Service provider, or worker: an independent person who offers to carry out tasks posted through the platform.
  • Request: the description of the task, duration, location and information the customer provides through the platform.
  • Booking: the agreement made electronically through the platform between the customer and the service provider to carry out a specific request.
  • Booking period: the number of hours for which the customer booked the service provider.

2. What Amil is

Amil is a technical platform that connects customers with independent service providers. It does not supply labour itself and is not a labour supply or labour operating company, unless expressly stated otherwise for a specific service.

The platform's role is limited to making it easier for platform users to search, communicate, book, pay and manage requests.

By using Amil alone, a service provider is not:

  • an employee of the platform;
  • its representative;
  • its agent;
  • its partner.

A service provider appearing on the platform is not a guarantee by Amil of any particular result, except to the extent the platform expressly states.

The service is carried out by the service provider according to the request and the agreement made with the customer through the platform.

3. Scope of services

The platform covers general tasks and services permitted by applicable regulations, such as:

  • moving or lifting items;
  • general cleaning;
  • tidying sites;
  • mowing grass;
  • simple garden work;
  • help with general physical tasks;
  • and other services the platform makes available.

Amil may not be used to request work that needs a licence or special professional qualification, unless the platform has made that category available lawfully and the service provider meets its requirements.

4. The relationship between customer and provider

When the customer accepts a service provider's offer or confirms a booking, the relationship for carrying out the task is formed between the customer and the service provider, according to the details of the request and the booking.

Amil is not a direct party to carrying out the task itself; it provides the technical infrastructure that helps both parties make and manage the arrangement.

Each party is responsible for the accuracy of the information they provide and for complying with the regulations and with the agreement made through the platform.

5. The customer's responsibility for describing the task

The customer must give a correct and clear description of the task before sending the request, including, depending on the nature of the service:

  • the type of work;
  • the place of work;
  • the area;
  • the number of workers needed;
  • the expected duration;
  • photos;
  • the nature of the items to be moved or handled;
  • and any risks or special conditions at the site.

If the actual work differs materially from the description given in the app, the service provider may refuse the additional work or ask, through the platform, to change the duration or value of the booking.

No additional amount may be charged to the customer without the customer's consent through the platform.

6. Booking by the hour

The service provider is booked for a set period that the customer chooses before confirming the request.

The booking value is due for the booked period, not only for the time the customer actually chose to use, because the service provider set aside the agreed period for the customer and could not offer it for other bookings.

So, if a customer books a service provider for five hours and the task ends after two hours, the early finish does not automatically reduce the booking value, and the agreed amount for the booked period remains due, unless the cancellation or refund policy or a specific offer says otherwise.

Example:

5 hours × 50 riyals = 250 riyals

If the work ends after two hours at the customer's request, or because there is no further work within the scope of the request, the booking total stays 250 riyals.

This condition is shown clearly to the customer before the booking is confirmed.

7. Going over the booked time

If the task continues past the booked period with the customer's consent, the extra time is charged at the rate applied to the booking, or at the rate shown to the customer before they agree to the extension.

The customer has 10 minutes after the booked period at no charge. If the work goes beyond those 10 minutes, a full extra hour is charged, even if it is exceeded by one minute.

Example:

The customer booked:

1 hour × 50 riyals

and the work continued for one extra hour with their consent. The total becomes:

2 hours × 50 riyals = 100 riyals.

Whenever possible, an extension should be confirmed in the app before the extra time starts.

A service provider may not extend the working time on their own in order to increase the value of the request.

8. When time starts and ends

Service time starts at the time set in the booking, or when the start of the task is confirmed in the app, according to the method the platform adopts.

The service provider must record the following in the app:

Arrival → Task start → Task end

The platform may use the electronic records, request data, timings, location and messages available to it to check disputes about service time.

9. Lateness

If the service provider is late for the agreed time, the late time may not be charged to the customer as working time.

If the service provider arrives on time and the work cannot start because of the customer, the site, or the task not being ready, the waiting time may be counted within the booking period, according to the policy shown to the customer when booking.

Before the service provider arrives, the customer is advised to keep valuable and sensitive items in a safe place and not to leave money, documents or high-value property unattended.

If any property is claimed to be lost, damaged or stolen, the customer must report it to the platform immediately and provide the information and evidence available.

The platform may suspend the service provider's account, investigate the incident and cooperate with the competent authorities whenever the law requires.

Nothing in this article affects any rights or responsibilities that the law places on any party.

If damage occurs, work must stop where necessary, and the incident must be documented and reported to the customer and the platform.

Claims are considered according to the circumstances of the incident, the available evidence, and the applicable regulations and policies.

Amil is not responsible for the service provider's work merely because the contact or booking was made through the platform, except to the extent that liability rests with the platform under applicable regulations or results from the platform's own error.

  • is unlawful;
  • differs materially from the description;
  • needs a licence or professional skill they do not have;

No fees that were not shown to the customer are charged unless the customer agrees to them, or they are due under announced terms the customer agreed to in advance.

The cancellation and refund policy applies subject to statutory rights that cannot be waived by agreement.
